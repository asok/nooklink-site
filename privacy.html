Privacy policy

Your live video and audio stay on your home network. This page explains what the app accesses, what remains on your devices, and when an internet connection is used.

Overview

Nooklink is a local baby monitor. An Android phone or iPhone captures video and audio; authorized viewers watch on another phone or the desktop app over the local network. Nooklink has no account system, cloud recording, analytics, or advertising in the app. The live stream is not sent to Nooklink servers.

Camera, microphone, and network

With your permission, the camera phone accesses its camera and microphone to create a live stream. The desktop app is monitor-only and does not capture camera or microphone input. The app uses local-network access to connect to authorized viewers. The viewer receives the stream only while the camera session is running. On Android, the camera can keep streaming after the screen is locked, with an ongoing notification; on iPhone, it stops when the app is paused. Operating-system and battery behavior can interrupt the stream.

Data stored on your device

The camera phone stores approved viewer credentials, and the phone or desktop monitor stores its last invitation for reconnecting. The apps use platform secure storage for these secrets. Imported sound files stay on the camera phone; viewers receive only their names and playback controls. Nooklink does not upload imported audio files or create cloud recordings.

Connections and sharing

Video and audio travel between the camera and authorized viewers over your local network using encrypted WebRTC media transport. Signaling messages are encrypted. Phone monitors can discover and pair with a camera after you compare a six-digit code; desktop monitors use an invitation code. Each invitation contains a secret, so share it only with someone you trust. You can revoke a viewer on the camera phone. Nooklink does not provide remote viewing over the internet.

Purchases and third parties

Purchases and restores are available only in the Android and iOS apps; the desktop monitor directs you to a phone to buy Pro. When a mobile build configures Nooklink Pro, it uses RevenueCat to retrieve offers and subscription status and to process purchases through Apple App Store or Google Play. These services may receive purchase, subscription, device, and technical identifiers needed for those functions under their own policies. This internet traffic does not include your live video or audio. Without a configured purchase key, the app does not initialize RevenueCat.

Your choices and deletion

You control camera and microphone permissions in your phone settings and can stop a session in the app. Revoke viewers on the camera phone before removing the app. Uninstalling may not remove every secure-storage credential or device backup, depending on the platform. For purchase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. Nooklink has no user account or server copy of your stream to delete.

Children and safety

The app may show a child in the live stream, but does not create a child profile or upload that stream. Nooklink is a support tool, not a medical device or a substitute for direct adult supervision. If the connection is lost or video stops, check on the child in person.

This website

This static website uses no analytics, advertising scripts, or cookies. Your language choice is saved in your browser's local storage. The site host may process ordinary web requests under its own privacy statement. The website is separate from the local video connection in the app.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email the Nooklink project at nooklinkapp@pm.me. We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes; the date above shows its latest revision.
